## Why Felbound exists

Between fights a warlock has a short list of things to keep in order: a demon out, Demon Armor up, a Healthstone in the bags, enough Soul Shards. Forgetting one of them shows in the next pull.

Felbound watches that list. When something is missing, a card appears. Click the card and Felbound casts the fix: it summons your demon, casts Demon Armor or makes the Healthstone. When everything is in order, the cards stay hidden.

## What it does

- **Your demon.** A card when your demon isn't out. Click it to summon the one you chose. [More](features/demon.html)
- **Demon Armor.** A countdown before Demon Armor (or Demon Skin) runs out, and a card when it is missing. Click to cast. [More](features/demon-armor.html)
- **Healthstone and Soulstone.** A card when there is none in your bags. Click to make one. [More](features/healthstone-and-soulstone.html)
- **Soul Shards.** A card when you are down to a few. [More](features/soul-shards.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: beside the game menu you get Ritual of Summoning, Unending Breath and your Hearthstone. [More](features/travel-panel.html)
- **Buff key.** One key that casts what the cards ask for. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Skins, layout and explanations.** Three looks, frames you can move and resize, and a short explanation in every tooltip. [Skins](features/skins.html), [Layout](features/layout.html), [Explanations](features/explanations.html)

Felbound only loads on warlocks.

## The Bound family

Felbound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core (LibBound): Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest) and Felbound (warlock). They look like their class but work the same way: the cards, the options window, the travel panel and the commands.
