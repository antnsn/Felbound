# Features

Felbound does a few small warlock jobs for you. Each page below covers one of them: what it does, how to use it, the settings in the `/fel` window, and the things that surprise people. New to the addon? Start with [your demon](demon.html) and [Demon Armor](demon-armor.html).

- [Your demon](demon.html): a card when your demon isn't out, click to summon it.
- [Demon Armor](demon-armor.html): a countdown before Demon Armor or Demon Skin runs out, and a card when it is gone.
- [Healthstone and Soulstone](healthstone-and-soulstone.html): a card when there is none in your bags, click to make one.
- [Soul Shards](soul-shards.html): a card when you are down to a few shards.
- [Travel panel](travel-panel.html): Ritual of Summoning, Unending Breath and your Hearthstone beside the Escape menu.
- [Buff key](buff-key.html): one key that casts what the cards ask for.
- [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html): a warning before they run out.
- [Skins](skins.html): Fel, Blizzard or Flat, to match the rest of your UI.
- [Layout](layout.html): moving and resizing frames, the grid, and the minimap button.
- [Explanations in tooltips](explanations.html): a short explanation on every card and button.
