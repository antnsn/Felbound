# Travel panel

Press Escape and a small **Travel** panel opens beside the game menu, with the warlock spells that help you and your group get around.

## What it does

The panel has three rows:

| Row | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Ritual of Summoning** | Brings a friend to you. Two more people have to help click the portal. You learn it at level 20. |
| **Unending Breath** | Lets you, or a friend, breathe under water for 10 minutes. You learn it at level 16. |
| **Hearthstone** | Takes you back to the inn you set it to. The row shows that place, for example "Hearthstone: Goldshire". |

- Click a row to cast it. The game menu closes after the cast.
- A spell you haven't learned yet is greyed out, with the level under its name. Its tooltip says where to learn it: "Learn it at level 20 from a warlock trainer."
- The Hearthstone row only shows while a Hearthstone is in your bags.
- The panel closes when a fight starts, and only opens out of combat.

## How to use it

1. Out of combat, press Escape.
2. Click the spell or the Hearthstone on the panel to the right of the game menu.

To change the inn your Hearthstone goes to, talk to an innkeeper.

## Settings

In `/fel`, page **Travel**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the travel panel when the game menu opens | On | Turns the panel on or off. |

## Good to know

**Can I move the panel?**
No. It always sits next to the game menu.

**The panel didn't show when I pressed Escape.**
It doesn't open in combat. Close the menu and press Escape again after the fight.
