# Healthstone and Soulstone

A Healthstone heals you a lot at once in a fight. A Soulstone lets you, or a friend, come back to life after dying. Felbound reminds you to make them between fights.

## What it does

- A card titled **No Healthstone** appears when there is no Healthstone in your bags and you know Create Healthstone.
- With the Soulstone reminder on, a card titled **No Soulstone** appears when there is no Soulstone in your bags and you know Create Soulstone.
- Click the card to make the stone. It uses the highest rank you know.
- The Healthstone comes first. When both are missing, the card asks for the Healthstone, then for the Soulstone once the Healthstone is in your bags.
- Any Healthstone counts, whatever its rank. The same goes for Soulstones.
- Each stone costs a Soul Shard. With no shard in your bags, the card says **Needs a Soul Shard** in red, and a click does nothing.
- The card stays hidden in combat.

## How to use it

1. Type `/fel` and open the **Stones & shards** page.
2. **Remind me to make a Healthstone** is on by default. Tick **Remind me to make a Soulstone too** if you want that one as well.
3. Out of combat, when the card shows, click it.

## Settings

In `/fel`, page **Stones & shards**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remind me to make a Healthstone | On | Shows the card when no Healthstone is in your bags. |
| Remind me to make a Soulstone too | Off | Shows the card when no Soulstone is in your bags. |

The Soul Shard settings on the same page are covered in [Soul Shards](soul-shards.html).

## Good to know

**I used my Soulstone on a friend and the card is back.**
The card only looks in your bags. Once the Soulstone has been used, it asks you to make a new one.

**Only one card for two stones?**
Yes. One card, Healthstone first, then the Soulstone.
