# Explanations in tooltips

Hover over a Felbound card or button and the tooltip says what it is for. Turn the explanations off once you know them.

## What it does

The explanations cover:

| Where | What it explains |
| --- | --- |
| [Demon card](demon.html) | What each demon does, and to summon it whenever it isn't out. |
| [Demon Armor card](demon-armor.html) | What Demon Armor and Demon Skin give you. |
| [Stone card](healthstone-and-soulstone.html) | What a Healthstone or Soulstone does, and that it costs a Soul Shard. |
| [Soul Shards card](soul-shards.html) | What shards are for and how to get them. |
| [Travel panel](travel-panel.html) | Ritual of Summoning, Unending Breath and the Hearthstone. |
| [Well-Rested and Well Fed cards](well-rested-and-well-fed.html) | Where the buff comes from and what it gives. |

On a card you can click, the tooltip shows the spell, the explanation, and "Click to cast (out of combat)".

## How to use it

Hover over a card or a row on the travel panel. To turn the explanations off or on:

- In `/fel`, page **Skin**, section **General**, untick **Explain things in tooltips**, or
- type `/fel explain`.

## Settings

In `/fel`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Plain-word help when you hover over a card or button. |

## Good to know

**What is left with explanations off?**
Tooltips on clickable cards still show the spell name and "Click to cast (out of combat)". The travel panel still shows the spell name and whether you have learned it. Cards that can't be clicked show no tooltip.

**No tooltip while the frames are unlocked.**
That's on purpose: unlocked cards are only for moving.
